### Sense gates

- [ ] Capability gap assessed: what the doers can do today vs what the new way needs
- [ ] Named users have done real tasks with the tool, hands on, more than once
- [ ] At least one champion identified who advocates without being asked

### Move gates
- [ ] Workflow redesigned around the AI step: what is removed, what is added, who signs off
- [ ] New way embedded in the real system of record, not running beside it
- [ ] Old way retired or formally changed
- [ ] Metric defined in the charter is being measured and has an owner
